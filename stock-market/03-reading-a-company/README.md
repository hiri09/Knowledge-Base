# Section 3 — Reading a company (fundamentals)

This is the part that decides what is worth owning. Slow, boring, and the only
section that compounds.

1. `financial-statements.md` — P&L, balance sheet, cash flow (cash is the honest one)
2. `key-ratios.md` — P/E, P/B, ROE, ROCE, D/E — the question each one asks
3. `valuation.md` — price vs. value, and why growth and rates swing it so hard
4. `moats-and-business-quality.md` — why some companies keep earning and others do not

**Done when** you can open any annual report and answer: does operating cash flow
track profit over five years, and why will this business earn more in five years?
