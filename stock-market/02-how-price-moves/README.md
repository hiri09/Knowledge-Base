# Section 2 — How price happens

Price is not a fact about a company. It is the last point two people agreed on.
This section is the machinery underneath that.

1. `order-book-and-order-types.md` — bid, ask, spread, market vs. limit vs. stop
2. `what-moves-price.md` — earnings, expectations, rates, flows, narrative
3. `market-participants.md` — who is on the other side of your trade

**Do in the lab after this:** place one market order and one limit order in
`../lab/trading-lab.html`. Compare the price you saw with the price you got.

**Done when** you can say why a stock falls on good results.
