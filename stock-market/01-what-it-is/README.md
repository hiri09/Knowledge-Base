# Section 1 — What the thing actually is

Before any chart, any ratio, any trade: what are you buying, from whom, and through
what plumbing. Nothing here is optional. Read in this order:

1. `what-is-a-share.md` — a slice of a business, not a lottery ticket
2. `primary-vs-secondary-market.md` — IPO vs. the exchange; where your money goes
3. `exchanges-and-brokers.md` — NSE/BSE, broker, demat, T+1 settlement, what you pay
4. `indices.md` — Nifty 50 and Sensex, and what the index number hides

**Done when** you can explain, out loud, what you own after buying one share, and
who got your money.
