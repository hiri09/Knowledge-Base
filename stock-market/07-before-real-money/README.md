# Section 7 — Before the first rupee

Two files. They are the gate between learning and investing.

1. `my-investing-thesis.md` — fill it in: amount, horizon, drawdown you can sit
   through, what you will and will not buy, and your buy checklist
2. `paper-trading-log.md` — the format for every practice trade, written *before*
   the outcome is known

**The gate:** 50 logged trades in `../lab/trading-lab.html` with written theses, an
emergency fund in place, no high-interest debt, and this thesis filled in.
Then, and only then, real money — and start smaller than feels interesting.
