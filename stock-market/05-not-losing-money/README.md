# Section 5 — Not losing money

The section everyone skips and the one that decides the outcome. You cannot control
returns. You control loss size and exposure.

1. `risk-and-position-sizing.md` — the 1% rule; the stop decides the size
2. `diversification-and-correlation.md` — ten stocks that fall together is one bet
3. `behavioral-traps.md` — the mistakes you will make even after reading about them
4. `fees-slippage-and-taxes.md` — the silent drag that kills active trading

**Do in the lab after this:** use the position sizer on every trade. Then trade the
"Sideways chop" scenario for 100 bars and read the *Fees paid* line.

**Done when** you never open a position without knowing the exit price first.
