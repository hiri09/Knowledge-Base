# Platform comparison — why the same video performs differently

Read this before assuming a piece can be cross-posted. It usually cannot.

## The one table that matters

| | YouTube | Instagram Reels | TikTok |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Algorithm rewards** | watch-through + downstream engagement | **saves + private sends** | completion + shares |
| **Distribution model** | a **library** (search + recommendation) | a feed, weighted by social graph | pure discovery feed |
| **Audience** | older, **intent-driven** | warmer, closer to your existing audience | broadest, youngest |
| **Content style expected** | information density | **polish, cinematic identity** | raw, unpolished |
| **Where reach comes from** | search + suggested, for years | Explore + existing followers | mostly non-followers |
| **Commercial intent** | high (people arrive looking) | **highest — closest to action** | lowest, entertainment-first |
| **Content lifespan** | months to years | days | days |

## What this means in practice

- *"A video that flops on TikTok might crush on YouTube Shorts because the educational angle appeals to YouTube's audience."*
- *"A casual talking-head video that crushes on TikTok might underperform on Reels if it looks too rough."*
- Reels is *"where TikTok's content goes to convert"* — narrower reach per post, but a significantly warmer audience.

## The rule

**Re-cutting for a platform is a different product, not a copy.** One research pass can feed every platform. One *edit* cannot.

What must change per platform: the opening seconds, the pacing, the visual finish, the call to action, and the metric you judge it by. What stays the same: the story, the facts, the point of view.

## Optimisation target per platform

- **YouTube** → optimise for watch-through. Write for someone who chose to be there.
- **Instagram** → optimise for **saves and sends**. Ask: would someone send this to a specific friend? If not, it will not travel.
- **TikTok** → optimise for completion and shares. Shortest possible version of the idea.

These are different objectives. A piece optimised for all three is optimised for none.
