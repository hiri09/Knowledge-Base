# YouTube rules

Applies to Shorts and long-form on YouTube only. Do not apply to Instagram.

## What the platform rewards

**Watch-through and downstream engagement.** Did they finish, and did they keep watching YouTube afterwards. YouTube treats content as a **library**, not a feed — search and recommendation surface videos for months and years.

**Consequence:** YouTube is the only platform where a back catalogue compounds. A documentary channel with 40 well-researched videos keeps earning from them. Write for the viewer who finds this in 2029.

## Tone and finish

- **Information density over polish.** The audience is older and intent-driven — they arrived looking for something. Give it to them faster than feels comfortable.
- Production value is not the goal. *"The best YOUTUBE videos possible"* beats the most beautiful ones.
- Titles should **pattern-match what already works in the sub-niche.** Novelty belongs in the content, not the title structure.
- Clarity beats cleverness in titles. The viewer must know what they are getting.

## Packaging

- **Title + thumbnail are decided before the script exists.** They set the expectation everything downstream must match.
- Packaging carries **~70% of CTR variance.**
- Generate 3–4 thumbnail variations; use YouTube's built-in thumbnail A/B test.
- Thumbnail: one readable emotion, one saturated element against a muted frame, text short enough to read at phone size.
- **The creator personally owns title and thumbnail.** This is the one role that is never delegated, in any well-run channel.

## Format specifics

| | Shorts | Long-form |
|---|---|---|
| Length | **50–60 sec** (best watch-through ~76%) | **12–17 min** for business documentary |
| Purpose | reach, hook testing, subscribers | watch hours, trust, money |
| Sub conversion | 0.05–0.5% | **2–5%** |
| RPM | low in every niche | where the money is |
| Counts toward 4,000 watch hours | **no, never** | yes |

**Shorts and long-form are separate ladders.** Shorts watch time never counts toward the watch-hours monetization path.

## Cadence

- Minimum viable: **5 Shorts/week.** Ideal for learning: 1/day for 30 days.
- Steady state: 3–5 Shorts/week + 1–2 long-form/week.
- **30–60 days of consistent posting** before the algorithm reliably recommends you.
- New channel day 1: **10–30 views is normal.** By day 30: 100–500/Short. Do not panic-change the format inside this window.

## Monetization gates

| Gate | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Fan funding | 500 subs + (3,000 watch hrs OR 3M Shorts views/90d) |
| YPP now | 1,000 subs + (4,000 watch hrs/12mo OR 10M Shorts views/90d) |
| YPP from Feb 1 2027 | 1,000 subs + (8,000 watch hrs/365d OR 20M Shorts views/90d) |

Channels already in YPP are grandfathered.

## Policy

- The **inauthentic content** policy judges the finished video, not how it was made. AI and faceless are not banned.
- Killed: generic, repetitive, mass-produced, templated-at-scale. A Jan 2026 wave terminated 16 channels for this.
- **Hard ban:** AI personas presented as human experts on health, legal, finance or politics.
- **Content ID cannot evaluate fair use.** It matches footage. A claim takes your revenue; **3 strikes ends the channel.** Analysis that argues *about* footage is the strong end; recap over studio footage is the weak end.
