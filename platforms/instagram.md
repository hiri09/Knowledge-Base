# Instagram rules

Applies to Instagram Reels only. Do not apply to YouTube.

## What the platform rewards

**Saves and private sends.** Not watch time, not likes. The question that decides whether a Reel travels:

> **Would someone send this to one specific person they know?**

If the answer is no, it will not spread, no matter how good it is. Everything below follows from that one fact.

## Tone and finish — the opposite of YouTube

- **Polish is rewarded here.** The audience is warmer and *"more forgiving of polish"* — a Reels-led brand can and should invest in a cinematic visual identity. The same cut that works raw on TikTok underperforms on Reels for looking rough.
- Instagram is a **visual identity platform**. Consistency of look across posts matters as much as any single post. The grid is part of the product.
- Higher contrast, cleaner type, deliberate colour grading, tighter motion. Make it look expensive.
- Where YouTube rewards information density, **Instagram rewards how it feels to look at.** Fewer facts, better presented.

## The audience

- Warmer, closer to your existing following, weighted by the social graph.
- Reach per post is **narrower** than TikTok — but the audience that does see it is *"significantly closer to taking action."*
- This is the conversion platform. Product launches, email signups and offers work here before they work on YouTube.

## Format

- Vertical, full-bleed. Nothing important in the bottom third — the UI covers it.
- Design for **sound-off first.** Captions are not optional.
- Cover frame is the thumbnail — it must work both in-feed and as a grid tile.
- A save-worthy Reel usually has a **reason to re-watch**: a number, a list, a comparison, something the viewer wants to show someone.

## Build for sends, concretely

- End on something **quotable or screenshot-able** — that is what gets sent.
- A single surprising number held on screen outperforms three competing facts.
- "Tag someone who…" is the lazy version. The real version is making the content itself feel like it belongs to a specific person the viewer knows.

## Judge it by

**Saves and sends.** Not views, not likes. A Reel with 10K views and 400 saves beats one with 100K views and 20 saves — the first one is building something, the second one happened to you.

## Relationship to YouTube

Instagram is **distribution and conversion**, not the home. Content lifespan here is days, not years. Never let Instagram's demands reshape the YouTube product — re-cut for it instead.
