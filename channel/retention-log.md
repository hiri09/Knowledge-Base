# Retention log

What the numbers said after publishing. Written by the Reviewer once a piece passes ~500 views.

**This file is why the system gets smarter.** Without it, video #50 is no better than video #1. The Researcher and Scriptwriter both read it before every cycle.

## How to fill it

Per `principles/attention-and-retention.md`:
1. Mark every point the curve drops **>5 percentage points in under 20 seconds**
2. Scrub there, watch 30 seconds either side, **name the cause in words**
3. Classify: opening cliff / mid-roll valley / end-of-content drop
4. **Change exactly one thing** before the next upload

## Standing lessons

Patterns confirmed across **multiple** uploads get promoted here. A single video's dip is noise — do not promote it.
