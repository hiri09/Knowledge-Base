## Cycle 1 — 2026-11-12

### 38 Studios / Kingdoms of Amalur
- **Status:** SURVIVED
- **The number:** $75M state-guaranteed loan; ~$28M lost by Rhode Island taxpayers; $61M court settlement
- **The decision:** Rhode Island's economic development board guaranteed a $75M loan to a first-time game studio run by a retired baseball pitcher, against 450 promised jobs
- **The human:** Curt Schilling — World Series pitcher, then games executive
- **The angle:** the game *succeeded* and the company still died. Amalur sold over 1M copies to warm reviews. It needed **3M just to break even.** The story isn't a bad game, it's a business that was never survivable at any realistic sales number.
- **Sources:** [Wikipedia: 38 Studios](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/38_Studios) · [GameSpot — investigation closed, no charges](https://www.gamespot.com/articles/four-year-investigation-into-kingdoms-of-amalur-de/1100-6442246/) · [Fortune — 4 lessons](https://fortune.com/2012/06/15/4-lessons-from-curt-schillings-38-studios-fiasco)
- **Scores:** demand 4/5 · pain 5/5 · fit 5/5 · angle 5/5 — **19/20**
- **Why it survives:** famous character, public money, SEC and FBI investigations, complete and fully-documented story, and an angle nobody leads with — it sold a million copies and still went bankrupt.

### CNN+
- **Status:** SURVIVED
- **The number:** ~$300M committed; shut down in **under five weeks**
- **The decision:** launch a subscription streaming service weeks before a merger that leadership knew was closing
- **The human:** Jeff Zucker (championed it, departed before launch); Chris Licht (killed it)
- **The angle:** this was not a product failure. CNN+ **could not legally stream the CNN feed** because of existing cable carriage deals — the one thing every subscriber assumed they were buying. It was dead at the whiteboard, not in the market.
- **Sources:** [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2022/04/21/cnn-streaming-service-pulls-a-quibi-will-shut-down-a-month-after-launch/) · [WCPO](https://www.wcpo.com/news/national/the-edsel-quibi-and-cnn-new-addition-to-business-failures) · [Cord Cutters News](https://cordcuttersnews.com/the-top-5-failed-streaming-services-including-cnn-quibi-more/)
- **Scores:** demand 3/5 · pain 5/5 · fit 5/5 · angle 5/5 — **18/20**
- **Why it survives:** the shortest major media product life on record, a structural flaw anyone could have spotted, far less covered than Quibi.

### Suicide Squad: Kill the Justice League
- **Status:** SURVIVED
- **The number:** **$200M write-down** (2024)
- **The decision:** Rocksteady — the Arkham studio — was pointed at a live-service looter shooter for nine years
- **The human:** Rocksteady leadership and WB's live-service mandate
- **The angle:** nine years of the best Batman studio in the world, spent building the thing its own audience had explicitly said it did not want.
- **Sources:** [Visual Capitalist](https://www.visualcapitalist.com/ranked-biggest-box-office-bombs-of-2024/) · industry write-down reporting, 2024
- **Scores:** demand 4/5 · pain 5/5 · fit 5/5 · angle 4/5 — **18/20**
- **Why it survives:** huge built-in audience, clean single-decision cause, complete story.

### Concord (Sony)
- **Status:** SURVIVED (lower rank)
- **The number:** $200M+ reported dev cost over 8 years (some reports $400M); **~700 peak Steam players**; pulled from sale 14 days after launch; Firewalk shut down
- **The human:** Sony's acquisition decision-makers
- **The angle:** Sony **bought Firewalk in 2023** — after the game was already deep in development and playable. The question isn't why Concord failed; it's what Sony saw before paying for it.
- **Sources:** [Wikipedia: Concord](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Concord_(video_game))
- **Scores:** demand 5/5 · pain 5/5 · fit 5/5 · angle 3/5 — **18/20**
- **Why it ranks lower:** heavily covered already. The acquisition angle is fresh but the story is not.
