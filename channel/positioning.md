# Channel positioning (locked)

The decision. Everything in `channel/` supports this; everything in `principles/` and `platforms/` is how it gets executed.

## What this channel is

**Business documentaries about entertainment money failures.**

"Why [Movie / Game / Show / Studio] Lost $200 Million." Budgets, box office, the decisions that burned the money, the people who saw it coming.

## Why this shape

| Decision | Reason |
|---|---|
| Entertainment **subjects** | mass appeal, global, insane built-in interest |
| Business **framing** | entertainment RPM is $1–3 (lowest tier). Business documentary is $5–14 |
| English, global audience | US viewers are worth 10–20x Indian viewers for identical content |
| Failure stories, not success | failure stories consistently outperform success on engagement |
| Faceless, voiceover + graphics | proven for this format; face can be added later |
| Numbers and charts, not clips | near-zero Content ID exposure. Analysis is the strong end of fair use |

**The core insight: the subject stays mass-appeal, the framing decides the RPM.** "What happened in the movie" is entertainment. "Where the money went" is business — same audience, 5x the rate, a fraction of the legal risk.

## Why it survives long term

- **Evergreen.** Trend videos earn 80–95% of lifetime views in 7 days. These keep earning for years. 40 well-researched videos is an income-producing asset.
- **Low-medium saturation** as a category — one of the healthier faceless niches, despite strong players at the top.
- **Self-replenishing supply.** New flops every year, forever.
- **Expands without resetting the audience:** movies → games → streaming → any business failure. The through-line "where did the money go" holds the audience through every expansion.

## The wedge

The established channels (MagnatesMedia, Company Man, ColdFusion, Why It Failed, Bankrupt Giants, TheCollapseCo) mostly cover **tech, retail and startups**. Entertainment-industry failures is the underserved corner, and it carries more built-in mass appeal than enterprise software.

## Sequencing

1. **Month 1** — Shorts only. Cheap reps, learn hooks, find which stories pull. Low stakes, high volume.
2. **Month 2** — first long-forms, built from the stories whose Shorts already performed. The Shorts are free market research for the documentaries.
3. **Month 3+** — 1 long-form/week plus its Shorts, from one batch research day.

**One research pass → 1 long-form + 6–8 Shorts.** The hard work happens once. This is what makes the system survive weeks with no time.

## Explicitly not doing

- **Not** plot recaps or reviews over studio footage — Content ID claims the revenue, strikes end the channel.
- **Not** multiple channels yet. Channels sharing an AdSense ID are investigated together; one inauthentic strike has killed three profitable channels at once. One channel to monetization first, then clone the system with a separate entity.
- **Not** chasing the Feb 2027 deadline. Unreachable from a standing start, and chasing it forces exactly the mass-produced output that gets channels terminated.
