# December 2026 sprint

Until January the goal is **retention data, not revenue.** Any agent optimising
for monetization in this window is solving the wrong problem.

**Himanshu is on leave Nov 30 – Dec 14 — no Claude, YouTube on phone only.**
He can publish and read analytics; he cannot produce. So everything ships before
Nov 30: ~12 Shorts uploaded and **scheduled to auto-publish ~1/day across the
leave.** Production needs him present, publishing does not, and a dark channel
stops getting tested.

**Measure retention, not views.** At 0 subs, view count on the first ~15 Shorts
measures whether the algorithm tested the channel, not whether the work is good —
it needs 20+ uploads to mean anything. AVP and the 3-second drop are readable off
50 views. This supersedes the 500-views threshold set on upload 002.

## The four gates (set 2026-11-14, before data)

Written in advance so January is a comparison, not a rationalisation.

| Gate | Pass |
|---|---|
| 3-second retention | ≥ 70% |
| AVP on a ~50s Short | ≥ 60% |
| Best single Short | > 5,000 views |
| Separation between hook arms | ≥ 10 points |

**3 of 4 → expand to long-forms. Miss → the problem is upstream of production.**

The last gate is the one that gets skipped: three hook arms within 10 points of
each other is an inconclusive test, not a winner. Re-run it.
