## The script (v4, approved)

**Beat 0 — Thesis Plant**
Jab koi giant company tumhari pasandida brand khareedti hai, aur promise karti
hai "hum isko waisa hi rehne denge" — us promise ki expiry date hoti hai. Aur
wo decide nahi hoti brand se. Wo decide hoti hai balance sheet se.

**Beat 1 — Origin**
1923. Chaar Warner brothers — Harry, Albert, Sam, Jack — ek studio banate
hain. Isi studio se aata hai Looney Tunes, classic Hollywood cinema.

**Beat 2 — The Empire Grows**
1996 mein, Warner Bros. merge hota hai Turner Broadcasting ke saath. Isse
aata hai CNN — aur Cartoon Network bhi — Pokémon, Tom & Jerry, Powerpuff
Girls. Saath hi HBO bhi isi chhatri mein — Game of Thrones, Friends.

2018 mein aata hai AT&T — America ka sabse bada phone/telecom company. AT&T
poori Time Warner ko khareed leta hai, John Stankey ko CEO banata hai. 2020
mein Stankey promote hoke khud AT&T ka CEO ban jaata hai — command jaati hai
Jason Kilar ke haath mein.

**Beat 3 — The Want**
Kilar ka sapna: HBO Max ko Netflix-Disney+ level ka giant banana. 2021 mein
poora saal ka movie slate — same din HBO Max pe release karta hai. Bold bet.

**Beat 4 — The Catalyst**
8 April, 2022. AT&T apna entertainment business WarnerMedia ko Discovery ke
saath merge karta hai — khud entertainment se bahar nikalne ke liye, apne
telecom karze pe focus karne ke liye.

Is deal ko structure karne ke liye, AT&T nayi company ke upar apna ~$43
billion ka debt aur obligations daal deta hai. Iske upar Discovery ka apna
financing add hota hai. Result: nayi company, Warner Bros. Discovery, apne
pehle hi din $53 billion ke gross debt ke saath shuru hoti hai.

Deal ki shuruaat se hi tay tha — combined company ka CEO hoga Discovery ka
CEO. Kilar chala jaata hai. Uski jagah leta hai David Zaslav.

**Beat 5**
Is merger se company ko milta hai $20 billion ka combined content-spending
power. Zaslav isi paisa se naya content banane ka vaada karta hai.

**Beat 6 — False Dawn**
2023 mein, isi studio se aati hai Barbie — globally $1.4 billion se zyada. Ek
second lagta hai — ye company kuch bada vision leke aayi hai.

**Beat 7 — The Decisions**
Pehla: Batgirl aur Coyote vs. Acme — dono Jason Kilar ke tenure mein greenlight
hui thi, fully complete thi — Zaslav unko release hi nahi karta, sirf tax
write-off ke liye.

Dusra: HBO Max ka naam "Max" 2023 mein, fir wapas "HBO Max" 2025 mein — koi
clear direction nahi.

Teesra, sabse bada nuksaan: Cartoon Network ka studio band, Boomerang band,
Looney Tunes ke episodes streaming se gayab.

**Beat 8 — Parallel Cut**
Zaslav ne Coyote vs. Acme ko "loss" bolke shelve kiya, public mein "courage"
bola. Dusri taraf — ek indie company usko 2026 mein release karti hai. Result:
$100 million se zyada.

**Beat 9 — Reckoning**
Cartoon Network ka studio khaali hai. Boomerang gone. Wo cartoons jo tum
dhoondte the — ab milte hi nahi.

**Beat 10 — The Forced Choice**
Company dooba nahi tha. Zaslav ke cost-cutting se, 2022 ke $53 billion ka
karza, 2026 tak ghat ke roughly $32 billion reh gaya tha. Numbers pe, company
better dikh rahi thi.

Par stock market isse alag nazar se dekh raha tha. Investors ko lagta tha —
WBD do alag companies hain, ek saath jodi hui: ek taraf HBO aur studios, jo
genuinely valuable hain — dusri taraf CNN jaisi linear cable networks, jo
dheere dheere mar rahi hain. Jab tak ye dono saath hain, poori company ki
value kam lagti hai.

June 2025, board khud decide karta hai company ko do companies mein split
karne ka — taaki har ek ki asli value alag se dikh sake.

Lekin yahi split announce hote hi, bahar ki companies dilchaspi dikhane lagti
hain. Board dus se zyada companies se baat karta hai. Aur jo shuru mein sirf
ek "split" tha — wo ban jaata hai ek poori bidding war.

Aur yahan wahi purani galtiyan wapas aati hain — Batgirl shelve karna, naam do
baar badalna, Cartoon Network ko todna — inhi sab ne brand ki trust itni
kamzor kar di thi ki stock kabhi real value pe wapas nahi aaya.

**Beat 11 — The Stamp**
Pehle Netflix maidan mein aata hai — poore WBD ke liye $83 billion,
specifically HBO Max ke liye. Board shuru mein isi deal ki taraf jhukta hai.

Paramount Skydance peechhe nahi hatta. Chhe alag offers deta hai. Jab board
nahi maanta, seedha shareholders ke paas jaata hai — hostile offer, $30 per
share, phir $31 per share.

Board declare karta hai — Paramount ka offer "superior" hai. Netflix wala deal
terminate hota hai — aur iske liye WBD ko Netflix ko $2.8 billion ka breakup
fee dena padta hai.

6 October, 2026 — $110.9 billion mein, poori Warner Bros. Discovery Paramount
Skydance ko bik jaati hai. 100 saal purana naam — ab sirf ek label hai.

**Beat 12 — The Echo**
Beat 1 yaad hai? Chaar bhai, ek naam jo Hollywood ki pehchaan bana. Ab wo naam
khud ek company nahi raha.

Aur paisa kahan gaya? Sirf 5 top executives ne combined $1.1 billion nikala is
sale se. Zaslav akela — $606.1 million. Sirf ek — JB Perrette — ko naye
Skydance mein koi role milta hai. Baaki sab bahar — paisa leke, bina kisi
zimmedari ke.

Hazaron logo ki naukri gayi. Tumhara bachpan ka Cartoon Network mit gaya. Aur
jisne ye sab decisions liye — wo akela insaan hai jiska is poori kahani mein
kuch nahi bigda.
