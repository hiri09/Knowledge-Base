**Title contract:** This Game Sold 1,000,000 Copies And Still Died
**Runtime:** ~46s · **Data:** `render/data/001-38-studios.json`

## Hook variants

1. **"This game sold a million copies. The studio still went bankrupt."** ← chosen
2. "A million people bought this game. It wasn't close to enough."
3. "Selling a million copies killed this studio."
4. "This studio needed 3 million sales. It got 1 million. It died in 90 days."
5. "A World Series pitcher bet a US state's money on this game."

**Chosen: 1.** Cleanest prediction error, pays the title contract in the first three seconds, and the proof arrives 25 seconds later so the loop stays open across the whole Short. Variant 5 leads with the character and is the strongest alternative — hold it as the A/B hook for Short 2.
